Module 01

Budgeting Basics

Give every dollar a purpose by understanding the building blocks of a monthly budget.

Income

Money you receive, such as wages, scholarships, allowances, or side-work earnings.

Example

Part-time work: $1,200/month

Fixed expenses

Bills that usually stay the same each month.

Examples

Rent, phone plan, loan payment

Variable expenses

Costs that change with your choices or needs.

Examples

Groceries, transport, entertainment

Needs, wants, savings

Prioritize essentials first, plan optional spending second, and pay your future self with savings.

A simple order

Needs → Savings → Wants
