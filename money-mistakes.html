Module 05

Money Mistakes

Small changes can protect your budget. Explore common student scenarios and practical fixes.

Impulse buying

Scenario: Jordan sees a sale and spends $60 meant for groceries.

Corrective action: Add a 24-hour pause and remove saved card details from shopping apps.

Ignoring small expenses

Scenario: Three small coffees a week quietly become more than $50 each month.

Corrective action: Track every purchase for one week and set a realistic “small treats” limit.

Late payments

Scenario: A missed phone bill creates a late fee and a stressful catch-up payment.

Corrective action: Use calendar reminders or automatic payments for recurring bills.

Unused subscriptions

Scenario: Casey pays for two streaming services they have not opened in months.

Corrective action: Review subscriptions monthly and cancel anything that no longer earns its place.

Spending without a plan

Scenario: Sam spends first and has no money left for an upcoming class fee.

Corrective action: Check the week ahead before spending and reserve money for known costs.
